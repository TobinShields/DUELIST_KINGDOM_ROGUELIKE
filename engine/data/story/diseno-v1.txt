DUELIST KINGDOM ROGUELIKE — DESIGN & CONTENT DATA v1.0
===========================================================
Project: circlenline / GOAT Format Simulator
Purpose: implementation-grade design brief + curated GOAT reward/card data + candidate character decks.
Language: systems/design notes in Spanish; official card names in English.

IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULE
-----------------------------
The simulator repository is the final authority. Before a card is shipped in Story Mode, resolve its exact
legal passcode against engine/data/goat-pool.json and its copy cap against engine/data/goat-limites.json.
pool_texts.json may contain alternate/GOAT/anime/action-field variants and therefore is NOT sufficient by
itself to prove story legality. Never silently add a modern or unavailable card.

The decklists below have been internally checked for 40-card main-deck size and April-2005 1/2/3 copy caps,
but Claude must run a repository-level validator over every final card/passcode before integrating them.

1. PRODUCT THESIS
-----------------
Duelist Kingdom should not be a linear anime campaign. It is a 30–45 minute, seeded, replayable deckbuilding
roguelike that uses Duelist Kingdom as its fiction and GOAT Format as its rules engine.

The fantasy:
- Start as Yugi with a recognizable, intentionally imperfect anime-style deck.
- Navigate a visible branching island map.
- Win and wager Star Chips.
- Open 10-card packs and keep every card in a temporary Run Binder.
- Constantly rebuild a legal 40–60 card deck.
- Gradually replace weak anime cards with premium staples and stronger thematic engines.
- Reach 10 Star Chips, enter Pegasus Castle, and finish with a deck that can plausibly resemble a good GOAT deck.
- Defeat Pegasus on Expert.
- Future clears unlock character mastery/passives and eventually additional playable duelists.

Core design tension:
"Do I keep improving the strategy I started with, or pivot because the run is offering me a better engine?"

2. NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN PILLARS
--------------------------------
1) GOAT legality is absolute. No Raigeki, Dark Hole, Monster Reborn, Change of Heart, Harpie's Feather Duster,
   or any other forbidden/non-pool card.
2) Character fantasy survives difficulty scaling. Weevil remains Insect; Mako remains WATER; Kaiba keeps
   Blue-Eyes; Joey keeps Red-Eyes; Pegasus M0 remains Toon/Relinquished-flavored.
3) Difficulty comes mainly from deck quality and competent AI, not from deliberately stupid early AI.
4) Normal rewards should almost never feel like three pieces of trash.
5) "Staple" is not synonymous with "auto-include." The loot system distinguishes universal power, premium
   interaction, and strategy-dependent engines.
6) Story rarity is an internal power/desirability tier, NOT printed Konami rarity.
7) The player chooses pack families. The game may react to the current deck only for guaranteed typed/synergy
   slots; it must never lock the player into the starter identity.
8) Run collection is temporary. Meta progression unlocks options/passives rather than permanent combat stats.
9) Existing VS Duel, Bot Mode, deck builder and ocgcore behavior must remain untouched/regression-tested.
10) No server, account, paid backend or external API is required.

3. RUN STRUCTURE
----------------
Target length: 30–45 minutes.
Structure: 3 Acts, each with approximately 6–8 traversed nodes. The complete Act map is visible from the start.
Not every node is a duel; target approximately 8–9 real duels in a full run.

ACT I — THE SHORES
- First two fights intentionally forgiving.
- Enemy Tier 1: anime-recognizable, functional, low staple density.
- Typical characters: Weevil, Rex, Mako, Joey.
- At least one Pack node and one utility node before the boss.
- No unavoidable Elite in the first two columns.

ACT II — THE ISLAND INTERIOR
- Tier 2 becomes the norm; selected Elites can use Tier 3.
- Characters: Mai, PaniK, Bonz, Bandit Keith, Labyrinth Brothers, stronger returning duelists.
- More recruiter engines, removal, traps and coherent win conditions.
- The player should normally be approaching 8–10 Star Chips here.

ACT III — PEGASUS CASTLE
- Entry requires castleQualified=true, earned by reaching 10 Star Chips at least once.
- Tier 3 / meta-adjacent opponents.
- Finalist pool can include Joey T3, Kaiba T3, Mai T3, Bandit Keith T3, Labyrinth T3, etc.
- Pegasus is always the final boss and always uses Expert AI.

Suggested node types:
DUEL / ELITE / PACK / CAMP / TRADER / EVENT / BOSS.

Map constraints:
- deterministic from seed;
- multiple meaningful routes;
- no impossible path to 10 chips if the player wins available opportunities;
- at least one Pack before every Act boss;
- at least one Camp or Trader in Acts I and II;
- no dead-end route created by insufficient Star Chips;
- track defeated characters to prevent nonsensical repetition;
- allow explicit repeat variants only when configured as narratively acceptable.

4. STAR CHIP ECONOMY — PROGRESSION + LIVES IN ONE SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------
Recommended v1 implementation:
- Start run with 2 Star Chips.
- Star Chips are both tournament qualification progress and the run's loss buffer.
- Standard Duel: stake 1. Win => +1 chip. Lose => -1 chip; node resolves and run may continue.
- Elite Duel: stake 1. Win => +2 chips + Elite rewards. Lose => -1 chip but still get the configured 1-of-3
  consolation reward.
- Act Boss: must be defeated to advance. Win => +2 chips. Loss => -1 chip and retry/route back to boss gate
  while chips remain.
- 0 chips => eliminated / run over.
- High-Stakes option: where enabled, stake 2 instead of 1. Win => +2; lose => -2. Never stake more than owned.
- Events may create +1, -1 or wager outcomes.
- Reaching 10 for the first time sets castleQualified=true permanently for that run.
- Once qualified, dropping below 10 in the castle does not eject the player.
- If player reaches the castle gate below 10 but above 0, generate deterministic Last Chance Duel branches
  until qualification remains mathematically possible. Never hard-lock a living run.
- Camp recovery restores exactly one chip that was actually lost earlier. Track recoverableLostChips.
- Display chips prominently as a Duelist Kingdom glove/progression object.

This preserves the anime reason for collecting 10 while making losses meaningful without forcing every single
loss to delete a 30-minute run.

5. PLAYER COLLECTION MODEL
--------------------------
- Active Main Deck: 40–60 cards, standard GOAT copy limits.
- Run Binder: temporary and effectively unbounded for v1.
- Run Fusion/Extra Binder: separate temporary collection.
- Player may open Deck Editor from the map at all times.
- Rewards enter Binder first; never auto-shove cards into Main Deck.
- Enforce legal max copies across the active deck, using the same rules/data as the existing deck builder.
- On run end, temporary Binder/deck are discarded; meta unlocks persist.

6. NORMAL DUEL REWARD
---------------------
Every normal Duel victory: show exactly 3 cards, choose exactly 1.

Generate the trio as:
A) SYNERGY: weighted toward current deck's dominant tags/type/engine.
B) QUALITY: generic competitive playable / staple.
C) PIVOT: adjacent or deliberately different strategy.

Quality floor:
- Outside explicitly labeled Bulk events, never intentionally generate three low-desirability cards.
- If all three candidates evaluate below rewardFloor, reroll deterministically from the same seed stream.
- Respect copy caps: a card already owned at its legal maximum should be removed/rerolled from normal rewards.

7. TEN-CARD BOOSTER PACKS
-------------------------
Player chooses one of FIVE broad pack families. Each pack has exactly 10 cards and the player keeps ALL 10.

Exact slot contract:
1–2) GLOBAL STAPLE slots: weighted from the premium generic pool.
3) TYPE MATCH slot: guaranteed Monster matching the current Main Deck's dominant monster Type.
   Tie-breaker: starter deck primary Type. If no legal candidate exists, use the closest strategy-tag Monster.
4–5) PACK CORE slots: chosen pack family.
6–8) GENERIC PLAYABLE slots: broad playable pool, avoiding low-value filler.
9) PIVOT/WILDCARD slot: adjacent strategy or a different pack family.
10) PREMIUM slot: high-rarity family card, Extra/Fusion card, or weighted global premium.

Anti-duplication:
- Don't offer cards the player cannot legally use because their owned quantity is already at the legal maximum,
  except inside the intentionally chaotic 40-card Bulk event.
- Pack duplicate copies are allowed only while the combined owned quantity remains legally useful.

Suggested staple-slot story-rarity weights:
Act I:  Rare 70% / Super 27% / Ultra 3%
Act II: Rare 55% / Super 37% / Ultra 8%
Act III: Rare 40% / Super 45% / Ultra 15%

Premium slot receives a separate family-biased table; Elite packs get an upgraded premium distribution.
These are tuning defaults, not sacred constants. Put them in data/config.

8. INTERNAL STORY RARITY
------------------------
Use four implementation tiers:
C = Bulk/Common utility; intentionally not part of routine premium reward screens.
R = Strong playable / role-player / recruiter / matchup card.
SR = Premium interaction / engine cornerstone.
UR = Jackpot / run-defining power.

Printed TCG rarity is irrelevant.
Banlist status influences availability but does not automatically determine story rarity.
Example: Swords of Revealing Light is Limited but should not have the same jackpot weight as Pot of Greed.

11. BULK EVENT
--------------
Special Event example: "Abandoned Card Trunk".
- Add 40 deterministic random C/Bulk-eligible legal cards to Run Binder.
- No guarantee that these are immediately good.
- Purpose: strange deck experiments + Trader fuel.
- This event is the explicit exception to the normal reward quality floor.
- Bulk pool must exclude forbidden cards and should heavily down-weight cards already at max useful copies.

12. TRADER — NO CURRENCY
------------------------
Trader replaces a normal shop. It converts excess Binder volume into directed quality.

Recommended v1 recipes:
A) BULK TRADE
   Sacrifice 10 C/Bulk cards -> choose 1 of 3 R cards.
B) FOCUSED TRADE
   Sacrifice 15 C/Bulk cards sharing Monster/Spell/Trap category -> choose 1 of 3 R/SR cards of that category.
C) PREMIUM TRADE
   Sacrifice 20 C/Bulk cards -> choose 1 of 3 SR cards.
D) PRESTIGE TRADE
   Sacrifice 2 SR + 2 R -> choose 1 of 3 UR cards. Maximum once per Trader visit.
E) EQUAL EXCHANGE
   Sacrifice 2 cards of the same story rarity -> choose 1 of 3 alternatives of that same rarity.
   Maximum once per visit.

All prices must be config-driven and Monte-Carlo tested. Mirror Force/Pot/Graceful-class cards should feel
expensive; the Trader must rescue bad loot, not guarantee a complete power-card suite every run.

13. CAMP
--------
At every Camp choose ONE:
1) FORTIFY
   +1000 starting LP for the next 3 Story duels.
   Non-stacking in v1; selecting again refreshes duration. Suggested hard cap: 10000 starting LP.
2) RECOVER STAR CHIP
   Restore exactly 1 previously lost Star Chip. Only available if recoverableLostChips > 0.
3) REFINE
   "Improve rarity" means transform value, not alter a printed card's physical rarity:
   sacrifice one owned C/R/SR card, then choose 1 of 3 offered cards from the next story-rarity tier with
   compatible CardType/tags. Respect copy limits.

14. EVENTS
----------
Tone: short character encounter, portrait + 2–3 choices; mechanically meaningful but not a visual novel.
Examples:
- Joey trade: swap one owned card for a Joey-tagged option.
- Grandpa advice: choose one of three Spellcaster/strategy cards or improve the next reward.
- Rare Hunter: risk one Star Chip for a premium reward.
- Weevil trick: accept temporary drawback for upgraded next reward.
- Star Chip wager: double-or-nothing duel/event.
- Abandoned Card Trunk: gain the 40-card Bulk bundle.
All event outcomes are seeded and data-driven.

17. ENEMY DIFFICULTY MODEL
--------------------------
Enemy = character identity + strategy + powerTier + AI difficulty.

Tier 1 = ANIME FUNCTIONAL
- recognizable signature cards;
- weak/nostalgic cards allowed;
- one or two real same-theme role-players;
- low density of jackpot staples.

Tier 2 = COHERENT ROGUE
- recruiter/search engine;
- useful removal;
- better curve;
- meaningful defensive suite;
- still strongly recognizable character deck.

Tier 3 = OPTIMIZED ROGUE / META-ADJACENT
- strong staples and interaction;
- coherent competitive engine;
- retains signature identity card(s), even if this is a small deckbuilding tax.

AI:
- first 1–2 duels: Normal/Tough depending on existing ladder naming;
- node 3 onward: primarily Tough;
- Act II: Tough/Expert;
- Act III: Expert;
- Pegasus: ALWAYS Expert.
Do not manufacture difficulty by making early AI intentionally nonsensical. Deck quality is the main progression.

18. PEGASUS BOSS PROGRESSION
----------------------------
Pegasus is ALWAYS the final boss.

Base / Mastery-0 clear:
- Pegasus M0 Toon Control candidate list below.
- Expert AI.
- High power/staple density while retaining Toon identity.

Next mastery:
- Pegasus M1 Relinquished Control.
- Expert AI.

Higher mastery:
Use EXISTING simulator meta decks by exact IDs/names from engine/data/mazos.json rather than duplicating them.
At the time of this design, useful existing names include:
- Goat Control · Worlds 2020
- Chaos Turbo · Worlds 2020
- Chaos Control
- Warrior Goat Control
- Reasoning Gate OTK
Additional existing decks may be eligible after repository audit.

Suggested bossMetaPool weighting:
M2: Chaos Control / Goat Control / Warrior Goat Control
M3+: Chaos Turbo / Chaos Control / Warrior Goat Control / occasional Reasoning Gate OTK
Always Expert. Seed determines selection.

19. SAVE / CONTINUE WITHOUT A SERVER
------------------------------------
Use browser localStorage for v1. Run state is small JSON and does not justify a backend.

Versioned keys:
- goat.story.meta.v1
- goat.story.run.v1
- goat.story.settings.v1

Persist:
- schemaVersion
- run seed
- deterministic PRNG internal state/counter
- generated map
- current node and resolved nodes
- selected character/starter/mastery/passive
- Binder card passcodes/IDs
- active 40–60 Main Deck
- run Fusion/Extra Binder
- Star Chips, castleQualified, lost/recoverable chips
- temporary Camp buffs and remaining duel counters
- defeated character IDs
- reward/event history required for determinism

Autosave after EVERY state-changing Story action.
Also add Export Progress JSON / Import Progress JSON for backup and cross-browser portability.
Warn that clearing site/browser storage deletes local progress unless exported.
Do not add IndexedDB unless later requirements exceed localStorage.

20. SEEDED RNG
--------------
Every run exposes a human-shareable seed such as DK-7HF82A.
Use a deterministic serializable PRNG (e.g. xoshiro128** or sfc32); never rely on unseeded Math.random() for
map, opponent, pack, event or reward generation.

Same seed + same content version + same player choices must reproduce:
- map
- encounter variants
- packs/rewards
- event rolls
- boss variant

Include contentVersion in seed/save compatibility logic.

21. UX DIRECTION
----------------
Keep the existing duel board for actual duels. Story Mode may have its own game-like shell:
- Duelist Kingdom map background
- path/node graph
- character portrait card on encounters
- Star Chip glove/progress
- pack-opening reveal
- Run Binder / Deck Editor shortcut always visible
- seed visible/copyable
- save/continue indicator
- responsive mobile layout

Do not bury card text. Mobile card inspection must remain readable.

22. BALANCE TARGETS — STARTING TARGETS, NOT PROMISES
---------------------------------------------------
Use automated bot simulations only as a tuning signal; they are not a substitute for human playtests.

Desired first-pass player experience:
- first two Act I fights: competent human target ~65–80% win chance with unmodified starter.
- late Act I / Act II: ~50–65% depending on route/build.
- Act III finalist fights: ~40–55%.
- Pegasus M0 first-attempt against an average successful run: ~30–45%.
- initial overall full-run clear rate target: roughly 20–35%, then tune from telemetry/playtests.
- no ordinary 1-of-3 reward where all three candidates are low desirability.
- successful runs should NOT routinely own every UR power staple before reaching the castle.
- every generated seed must contain at least one reachable route to 10 Star Chips.
- target ~8–9 actual duels in a complete run to protect the 30–45 minute goal.

Create a headless/story simulation script that samples thousands of seeds and reports:
- node distribution
- combat count
- theoretical obtainable Star Chips by route
- pack rarity output
- duplicate/reroll rates
- Trader conversion rates
- number of UR/SR cards entering a typical Binder
- impossible/deadlocked maps (must be zero)
- average route length

23. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------
Before Story content is accepted:
1) Resolve every named card to an exact repository passcode.
2) Assert it is in goat-pool.json.
3) Assert legal copies against goat-limites.json.
4) Reject anime/action-field/nonstandard variants unless explicitly approved and legal.
5) Validate every candidate deck has 40–60 Main cards.
6) Validate each enemy Extra/Fusion card exists in the repository.
7) Validate all pack/reward pools have enough candidates after copy-cap filtering.
8) Generate a story-validation-report listing missing cards, substitutions and warnings.
9) Never silently substitute a missing card.
10) If substitution is necessary, choose a GOAT-legal same-role replacement and record the change.

27. COMPETITIVE RESEARCH SYNTHESIS — WHY THE LOOT TABLE IS BUILT THIS WAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This section explains the competitive logic behind the reward design. It is not intended to freeze the format
into one historical decklist; it distills recurring principles from modern Goat play and documented rogue decks.

A. THE 2026 COMPETITIVE CORE
- GGP Ludwigshafen 2026 drew 202 players. Coverage explicitly describes Chaos Control as resurging in response
  to a metagame shaped heavily by Chaos Turbo and Warrior.
- GFCEU 2026 reported a Top 8 consisting entirely of Chaos Control, Warrior and Chaos Turbo, while the Top 16
  still contained less-standard strategies such as Gumo, SWAG Cat and Soul Control.
- GGP Buenos Aires 2026 was won by Chaos Turbo in a 134-player field; another top-cut list blended Chaos Turbo
  and Chaos Control concepts with Scapegoat/Metamorphosis.
Design implication:
  The Story Mode's strongest generic rewards must support the fundamental GOAT axes of card advantage,
  flexible removal, LIGHT/DARK conversion, Warrior pressure and Goat/Metamorphosis control. However, a run
  should not simply hand the player a completed Chaos deck.

B. "STAPLE" DOES NOT MEAN "ALWAYS MAIN-DECKED"
- GoatFormat strategy material explicitly discusses cards such as Book of Moon, Heavy Storm, Mystical Space
  Typhoon, Nobleman of Crossout, Scapegoat, Snatch Steal, Call of the Haunted, Dust Tornado, Mirror Force and
  Sakuretsu Armor as powerful cards that can nevertheless be sided out in particular matchups.
- Chaos Turbo discussion notes that even cards commonly regarded as staples, such as MST or Mirror Force, can
  be omitted by successful lists.
Design implication:
  Story rarity should measure expected run value, not "must play in every deck." A large reward-worthy pool
  is healthier than a tiny list of automatic inclusions.

C. SCAPEGOAT / METAMORPHOSIS IS AN ENGINE, NOT TWO ISOLATED CARDS
- Goat Control is fundamentally associated with Scapegoat + Metamorphosis into Thousand-Eyes Restrict.
- Chaos Control demonstrates that the package can be transplanted into a LIGHT/DARK shell.
- Fusion guides show that Metamorphosis creates a whole level-dependent toolbox: Dark Balter, Fiend Skull
  Dragon, Ryu Senshi, Last Warrior, Gatling Dragon and others.
Design implication:
  Main-deck and Fusion rewards must talk to one another. The game should recognize when Meta has no useful
  target and increase compatible Fusion weighting, rather than presenting dead "premium" loot.

D. CHAOS TURBO SHOWS WHY ENGINE CARDS CAN BE AS EXCITING AS POWER SPELLS
- Thunder Dragon, Night Assailant, Dekoichi/Magical Merchant, Card Destruction and discard traps create a
  coherent value engine that enables Chaos monsters.
Design implication:
  Thunder Dragon or Night Assailant should not be valued like random filler. They belong in strategy-aware
  reward tables even though they are not universal staples.

E. RECRUITERS ARE A MAJOR ROGUELIKE BRIDGE
- Documented Zombie, WATER, Insect, Harpie/WIND, Dragon and Earth shells repeatedly use recruiters such as
  Pyramid Turtle, Mother Grizzly, Flying Kamakiri #1, Howling Insect, Shining Angel, Mystic Tomato, Giant Rat
  and UFO Turtle.
Design implication:
  Recruiters are excellent mid-tier rewards: useful immediately, identity-forming, and capable of pulling the
  player toward a new strategy without needing one specific jackpot.

F. ROGUE SUCCESS IS REAL, NOT PURE ANIME FANTASY
- Modern coverage has documented WATER, Earth, Machine/XYZ, Blue-Eyes/Dragon and other non-core strategies in
  large events.
- Ben Kei OTK has historical success and a modernized version has continued to receive attention; its deck
  illustrates how searchers, stall and Equip cards create a legitimate alternate win route.
Design implication:
  Packs should contain credible build-around cards and not merely cosmetic anime references. "Weird run"
  should mean a coherent rogue engine, not a deck made from bad cards.

G. CHARACTER TIER DESIGN
Tier 1 intentionally tolerates anime inefficiency.
Tier 2 borrows proven structural ideas: recruiters, searchable engines, reliable battle/removal traps.
Tier 3 can borrow heavily from documented rogue shells and the format's true power package, but keeps a visible
character signature even when that signature is a deckbuilding tax.

H. REWARD PSYCHOLOGY
The strongest dopamine moments should come from several different categories:
- raw card advantage jackpot: Pot of Greed / Graceful Charity;
- flexible removal/tempo jackpot: Mirror Force / Ring / Torrential / Snatch;
- engine completion: Scapegoat + Metamorphosis + Thousand-Eyes Restrict;
- strategy unlock: Chaos Sorcerer/BLS after LIGHT-DARK density becomes real;
- tribal upgrade: Abyss Soldier, Pyramid Turtle, Limiter Removal, Kycoo, ROTA, etc.;
- Fusion toolbox completion.
This makes "one more node" attractive without requiring every reward to be an objectively broken Limited card.
